## Why this blog

Some of my free time goes into [Hack The Box](https://app.hackthebox.com/). I play the seasonal machines (Seasons 11 and 12 are on here), work through Sherlocks, and pick off challenges across pwn, crypto, reverse, web, forensics, coding, OSINT and misc.

The two sides feed each other. Breaking into a box shows me what an attacker's path looks like from the inside, and the Sherlocks are the same incidents seen from my side of the SOC: a pcap, a pile of `.evtx` files, a `$MFT`. That's why every machine writeup here ends with a remediation section and the Sherlocks end with detection notes. Getting root is half of it; the other half is knowing which control would have stopped it.

## How I write these

These started as my own HackMD notes, so they read like notes: what I tried, what didn't work, where I got stuck, and sometimes a complaint about a "Very Easy" box that wasn't. I'd rather keep the dead ends in, since they're usually where I learned the most.
